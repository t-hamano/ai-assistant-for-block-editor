Why we moved to a four-day week

Last spring, Acme tried a four-day week for three months. Nobody was sure it would work.

What changed

Meetings were cut in half, and every team agreed on two focus days without calls.

  • Fewer meetings
  • Shared focus days
  • Clear weekly goals

Pricing

The trial cost Acme nothing extra, because the same work fit into four days.

What comes next

Acme will keep the four-day week and review it again next year.
