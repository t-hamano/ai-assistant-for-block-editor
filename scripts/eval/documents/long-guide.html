A year in the kitchen garden

This guide follows one small kitchen garden through a whole year. Each section covers one crop: when to sow it, how to care for it, and when to pick it.

Tomatoes

Tomatoes are one of the crops our readers ask about most. In our beds, tomatoes take about 8 weeks from sowing to the first harvest, and they do best in a spot with at least 5 hours of sun.

Sowing tomatoes

Sow tomatoes indoors in early spring. Keep the seeds about 1 centimeters deep, and thin the seedlings to 10 centimeters apart once they have their first true leaves.

Watering tomatoes

Water tomatoes deeply about 2 times a week in dry weather, early in the morning, so the leaves dry before evening. Uneven watering is the most common reason tomatoes crack, bolt, or taste bitter.

  • Check the soil around the tomatoes with a finger before watering
  • Water the tomatoes at the base, not over the leaves
  • Give the tomatoes more water while they set fruit or roots

Harvesting tomatoes

Pick tomatoes when they are fully colored and slightly soft. Regular picking keeps the tomatoes producing for longer, and stored in a cool place they keep for about 2 weeks.

In our notes from last season, the tomatoes gave about 3 kilograms per square meter, a little more than the year before, mostly because we started them a week earlier.
